Business Model Canvas

Who benefits, what value do they get, and what would it take to deliver the service? We chose the Business Model Canvas.

Value Propositions

What value do we deliver? Which needs do we satisfy?

  • Stickers, fabric patches and other products made by the entrepreneur
  • Overall patches (haalarimerkit) for students
  • With the redesign: a smaller navbar, search that works and clearer pages

Customer Segments

For whom are we creating value?

  • Mostly young people who appear to be women (from social media comments)
  • Students buying overall patches, like our Laurea persona

Customer Relationships

What relationship does each segment expect?

  • Personal contact through social media and comments
  • Self-service web shop

Channels

How do customers want to be reached?

  • The web shop
  • Social media
  • Events
  • Delivery and after-sales

Key Partners

Who are our key partners and suppliers?

  • Material suppliers
  • Payment and delivery providers

Key Activities

What activities does our value proposition require?

  • Designing and making the products
  • Running the web shop and its events page
  • Social media content

Key Resources

What resources does our value proposition require?

  • The entrepreneur's own designs and brand
  • The web shop
  • The social media following
  • Equipment and money

Cost Structure

What are the most important costs in our business model?

  • Materials and production
  • Web shop platform and hosting
  • The entrepreneur's working time

Revenue Streams

For what value are customers really willing to pay?

  • Sales of stickers, fabric patches and other products through the web shop
  • Other income, such as sales at events
